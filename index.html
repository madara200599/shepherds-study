        How it works & why you can trust it

        • AI drafts, humans decide. The outline, questions and prayer points are a starting draft. A pastor reviews every word before it reaches a congregation.
        • Scripture stays Scripture. Verses are quoted from the public-domain KJV and shown with their reference, so anyone can verify them in seconds.
        • Works offline too. If the live AI service is unreachable, a built-in study engine (curated verse library + sermon structure) produces a complete study on your device. Nothing you type leaves the browser in offline mode.
        • No accounts, no tracking, no cost. No sign-up, no data collection, no payment. Built for the AI for Church Hackathon 2026 (Oct 30 – Nov 27, 2026).
